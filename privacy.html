The website

galadin.eu is a website of Galadin, a small independent studio from the Netherlands. It has no cookies, no statistics and no ads, and you do not need an account. The website is hosted by GitHub Pages. Like any web server, GitHub may process technical data such as your IP address when a page is requested; we have no access to that. See GitHub's privacy statement.

The games

The games run entirely in your browser and send nothing to us or to anyone else. Your progress and settings are stored in your own browser's storage, on your own device. Clear this website's data in your browser and the progress is gone. Your language choice is stored the same way (key galadin_taal); it goes nowhere and is not used to track you. Some games let you load a mod from a file or from pasted text on your own device; it stays on your device and nothing is uploaded.

The games on this website are for an older audience. 12+, 16+ and 18+ are our own age labels, not a PEGI or USK rating; check the label on each game. Some games contain violence, or simulate gambling with play money (nothing real can be won or bought). Parents and carers decide what is suitable.

We hold no data about you. What is on your device, you remove through your browser settings.
